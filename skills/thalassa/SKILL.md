# THALASSA hydrological and aquatic evidence pack
Version: 1.0.0. Instruction competence is not independently validated model competence.

## Quantity and support checks
Distinguish precipitation, soil/root-zone water, surface storage, groundwater, discharge, stage, concentration and mass load. State depth, catchment, station/support, units, period and actual measurement method. Rainfall and regional gravity cannot establish a local well or root-water condition.
Concentration multiplied by compatible flow can describe load; neither may be substituted without actual matched observations. Salinity, temperature and oxygen support depend on depth and sampling time.

## Conservation and readiness
Write the control volume and all boundary inputs/outputs. Include snow as internal transfer when its store is inside the volume. Never subtract total ET and transpiration twice. The registered bucket checks conservation in declared mm/step and remains illustrative; it is not calibrated river routing, Richards flow, groundwater or treatment efficacy.
Routing, hydraulic, coastal and chemical transport require their actual solver, boundary conditions, parameters, observational support and untouched validation. A statistical climate response is not discharge or water-safety certification.

## Distinguishing observation
Compare upstream/downstream/reference flow or root-zone moisture at matched timing, together with catchment rainfall and recorded drainage/withdrawals. Document station history, method and upstream lineage. The same gauge accessed through CUAHSI and another portal is one observation lineage.
For safety/restoration questions, identify an appropriate qualified sampling/laboratory protocol. Do not certify potable water, recommend chemical treatment or promise ecological recovery.
