# CHLORIS plant and agriculture evidence pack
Version: 1.0.0. Listed sources and protocols are not evidence that an adapter or process model has passed validation.

## Observation identity
EVI/NDVI are optical greenness proxies, not direct species, yield, biomass, tree mortality, infection or restoration measurements. Preserve actual collection, scale factor, fill, quality flags, snow/cloud selection, native footprint and acquisition/composite timing.
For MOD13Q1 distinguish 16-day composited observations from daily measurements. Use the registered QA-filtered growing-season recipe; an annual aggregate needs yearly dependence settings, not compressed monthly HAC.
Compare phenology/growth stage, cover, management and changing accepted pixel support. Remote context cannot authenticate a farmer's diagnosis.

## Competing explanations
Keep heat/water limitation, fire/disturbance, soil/drainage/root injury, pests/pathogens, management and retrieval changes distinct. Require crop/species, location, onset, spatial pattern, growth stage and relevant measurements before recommending a diagnostic contrast.
An actual greenness trend may support a phenotype signature without proving its cause. Missing plant measurements are a gap, even when weather records are complete.

## Safe next step
Use consented photos/history, reference versus affected areas, root-zone moisture, documented disturbance/intervention, and qualified diagnostic sampling. Describe observable contrasts and timing; do not invent laboratory findings or pesticide doses, recommend indiscriminate removal, move suspect material contrary to rules, or promise recovery of dead plants.
Prepare an evidence packet and verify an actual local extension/laboratory contact before a user-approved handoff. No autonomous contact or private-data transfer.
