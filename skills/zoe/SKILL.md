# ZOE animals and biodiversity evidence pack
Version: 1.0.0. This pack does not make occurrence records a census or a language model a diagnostician.

## Evidence identity and effort
Distinguish occurrence, abundance, detection/nondetection, survey absence, individuals, movement, contact, exposure, infection and mortality. State species/taxon, method, effort, spatial/temporal support, observer/producer and permissions.
Missing records with missing survey effort do not establish absence or decline. NASA habitat/weather/greenness measurements are context, not animal counts or pathology.
Shared observations distributed by multiple portals remain the same lineage. Protect sensitive species locations and private animal/farm records.

## Mechanism and model limits
Ask what animal outcome was actually observed and where/when it occurred. Separate habitat/food/water change, fire, movement, toxicity, infection and sampling/reporting alternatives.
Movement requires actual species/behavior/locations. Exposure requires contact/release/support information. Transmission requires actual contact and infection measurements plus an appropriate validated model; wind, greenness or nondetection cannot substitute for these inputs.
Do not replace an unavailable animal process model with a drought or climate model, or attach a causal probability to a hypothesis.

## Useful safe observation
Plan effort-recorded affected/reference surveys and, where appropriate, qualified veterinary/wildlife pathology checks. Give observable contrast, support, timing and remaining ambiguity. Do not direct unsafe handling, capture, specimen transfer or treatment; escalate to qualified local authorities and obtain consent before any external handoff.
