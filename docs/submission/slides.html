04 · THE ANSWER

Monthly evidence: inconclusive

The 2001–2024 monthly EVI trend is not inferentially resolved.

Most months fail the registered spatial QA coverage threshold. Missingness is not repaired into an apparently continuous curve.

This supports neither a confident increase nor a confident decrease for the polygon-level landscape greenness series.

Not measuredforest-only changebiomass or tree abundancedisturbance attribution

EVI is a landscape optical greenness proxy. It is not biomass, mortality, biodiversity, or a forest-only measurement.

06 · KEEP THE TWO SIGNALS SEPARATE

Positive descriptive EVI slope ≠ resolved ecological recovery

July–September EVI: 21 of 24 complete seasons; +0.006126 EVI/decade, descriptive only.

No confidence interval or p-value is available under the registered gap rules. Related climate and vegetation views are not independent corroboration; a short seasonal comparison cannot replace the unresolved monthly record.

The 2024 seasonal EVI value is below 2001. A fitted positive slope is not a claim that every year increased.
