Question and data

What changed, and what remains unmeasured?

Example: British Columbia interior plateau, 53.91° N, 122.75° W; January 2001–December 2025.

Three actual NASA POWER monthly variables: air temperature, relative humidity and precipitation rate. Each contains 300 observations.

These are MERRA-2-derived, grid-supported estimates—not local stations, watershed means or direct measurements of plant stress or animal mortality.

Precipitation is a mean daily rate, not monthly rainfall total. Separate mission-data verification acquired 25 annual MODIS EVI values for Alberta Mixed Grass Prairie: exact Collection 6.1 granule identity, raw QA, five unverifiable revisions excluded. The distinct footprint is not a matched BC ecosystem.

Observed series

Seasonality and change are separate questions

Seasonal ordinary least squares with elapsed years and calendar-month effects; Newey–West HAC, Bartlett bandwidth 12, nominal 95% slope interval. Sensitivity: bandwidths 6 and 24.

Missing periods are not compressed or imputed for regular-HAC inference. Trend and significance do not identify a cause.
