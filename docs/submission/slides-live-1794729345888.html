METHOD, NOT MODEL-INVENTED NUMBERS

Magnitude and uncertainty are separate.

Registered seasonal ordinary least squares with calendar-month effects. Bartlett/Newey–West heteroskedasticity-and-autocorrelation-consistent uncertainty, with bandwidth sensitivity.

Actual elapsed time, source units, complete calendar and missing periods are preserved. Short, constant or incomplete series do not acquire unsupported significance labels.

Intervals shown here are per year; headline changes are per decade. Precipitation in mm/day is a mean daily rate, not accumulated rainfall.

Full specifications, diagnostics, assumptions and limitations accompany every exported artifact. Newey & West (1987), doi:10.2307/1913610.

ADAPTIVE INVESTIGATION

Specialists are selected, not a checklist.

GAIA owns the conversation. AURA investigates atmosphere; CHLORIS plants; THALASSA water; ZOE observation-aware animal evidence. Only relevant roles run.

Retrieved literature informs competing explanations; actual measurements determine what can be tested. Shared-source dependence, mismatched support, contradictions and missing outcomes remain visible.

Recommendations identify observations that could change the interpretation. Weather association is not an ecological diagnosis or proof of cause.

Illustrative simulations are labeled, not promoted to validated predictions. Unavailable specialized solvers are explicitly unavailable.
