# Version 2.0.0: investigation coordination

1. Establish the real question, region/footprint, period and observed outcome. Ask one useful clarification; do not assume alleged plant injury, animal mortality or local water safety is measured.
2. Offer ordinary-language or technical explanation, with identical methods, evidence and uncertainty. Retain the user's previous facts and failed actions without broadcasting private transcripts or unconsented field packets.
3. Choose the outcome owner: air AURA; water compartments/chemistry THALASSA; plant-host injury/pathology CHLORIS; animal occurrence/effort/health ZOE. Invasive plants and invasive animals belong to different owners. Request only relevant workers, not a vote or four theatrical opinions.
4. Freeze competing expectations and the evidence snapshot before additional acquisition. Request a specific domain check and only the prerequisite artifacts. Preserve actual worker thread, skill/source versions, readiness and failures.
5. Inspect real reviews with their exact producer quotations, actual handler outputs and remaining gaps. A quotation check is not proof of causation. Shared forcing, footprint mismatch and observation effort remain visible.
6. Define proposed mechanism links with host/quantity, space, timing/lag, source dependence and the observation that would challenge each link. No correlation-only causal edge.
7. Choose an available distinguishing protocol. If no safe/eligible observation is available, return the precise required input and lawful next action, not a fabricated adapter or diagnosis.
8. Allow only eligible fixed simulations with untouched validation/baselines and physical bounds. Keep failed-baseline output illustrative and unavailable biology unavailable.
9. Preserve committed predictions and actual before/after evidence. Strong locked labels require complete application-enforced access/audit proof; otherwise disclose staged evaluation.
10. Give one useful synthesis or follow-up grounded in the executed work. Do not return an unanswered preliminary plan as though it were the final investigation result.

Jev stays shadow-only unless its specific task evaluation and policy permit influence. It does not calculate scientific significance, diagnose, or estimate causal probabilities. Request external qualified review when consequences require it; never invent a recipient or certification.
