# Version 2.0.0: common scientific boundary

## Activation and exclusions
Load before reviewing any investigation. This pack never grants filesystem, credential, network, peer-agent or executable-code permission. Supporting sources must be requested by their registered IDs through the trusted reference loader.

## Ordered checks
1. Identify the requested outcome and separate user reports from producer observations, model outputs, optical proxies and professional diagnoses.
2. Identify quantity, units, observation window, native support, posted grid, QA, method and lineage. Unspecified values stay missing. Resampling does not create new observational resolution.
3. Inspect actual owned artifacts, not memory or another worker's confidence. Preserve fill values, exclusions and actual calendar time. Never compress gaps or invent significance.
4. Load the source relevant to the claim. Quote its actual retrieved text and return the registered source ID. A matching quote verifies quotation, not every paraphrase or causal interpretation.
5. Check space, season, observation/report timing and dependence before linking sources. Multiple products sharing a sensor/forcing and duplicated records do not multiply corroboration.
6. Use fixed numerical handlers only. Respect required identities, units, physical bounds, chronological partitions and reserved targets. A successful calculation does not validate its scientific interpretation.
7. Record contradictions, missing inputs, alternative observation processes and the next useful distinguishing measurement. No forced causal winner or impressive reversal.
8. Return qualitative review, owned artifact IDs, exact source quotations, unresolved prerequisites and a protocol. All numeric empirical results remain in the numerical artifacts, not invented prose.

## Stop and escalate
Missing quantity/support, mismatched source, incomplete domain prerequisites, unsafe intervention, uncertain diagnosis or missing professional qualification means descriptive/protocol/review-only output. Never remove the uncertainty label to satisfy the user. Model, source-access, numerical-test and domain-evaluation readiness are separate dimensions.

## Version and reviewer
Developer-reviewed procedures and producer excerpts. Internal tests are not independent scientific or clinical certification. Changes require source and case-regression review.
