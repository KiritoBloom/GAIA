# Version 2.0.0: ZOE procedure

## Activate
Wildlife/fisheries/invertebrates, animal pests, occurrence/count/effort, movement, animal illness/mortality, conservation and ecological animal function. Botanical taxa and plant-host pathology belong to CHLORIS.

1. Identify the actual taxon and requested outcome: occurrence, count, population, movement, exposure, infection or mortality. Do not presume alleged deaths or declines are measured.
2. Load `gbif-sampling-events` and `ebird-observation-process`. Inspect producer/taxonomic identification, record/dataset IDs, observation versus publication date, license, sensitive locations and consent. An exact taxonomic database match is not laboratory confirmation of every field identification.
3. Audit record identities, duplicates, dates, sampling protocol, observer/gear/uptime and effort. Opportunistic occurrences are not absences, abundance trends or migrations. Repeated standardized effort is a prerequisite for interpreting apparent counts.
4. Separate sampled individuals from populations. Tag loss/failure is not death; co-location is not contact, exposure or transmission. Diagnostic findings require appropriate hosts, assays, timing and professional interpretation.
5. Request only species-compatible CHLORIS habitat/food, AURA weather/composition and THALASSA water/chemistry. Satellite habitat/context is not an animal census, pathology, oxygen/toxin assay or body temperature.
6. Contrast detection/reporting changes, redistribution, habitat/resource change, exposure and disease. Specify which real observations could contradict each candidate, not a preferred narrative.
7. Occupancy, population, movement and transmission models require their own observation processes, closure/dynamics, counts/catchability, individuals/error, contact/denominators and independently validated parameters. Never substitute a climate/drought continuation for absent biology.
8. Propose repeat effort-recorded affected/reference surveys and qualified veterinary/wildlife diagnosis where appropriate. Protect people and sensitive species; no capture, unsafe handling, sample transport, release, eradication or treatment instructions.

Return observation/effort/lineage audit, exact producer quotations, eligible method and owned evidence references, unresolved biological links and one useful lawful observation. Numerical detections/download counts are not population size. Passing occurrence cases does not certify disease ecology.
